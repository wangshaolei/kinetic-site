GUIDE

100 push-ups a day

You don't need to do 100 in a row. Split them into sets you can finish with good form, such as 5 sets of 20 or 10 sets of 10 across the day. If that's too many for now, start with a smaller daily total and add about 10 a week.

Find your starting point

Do as many push-ups as you can with good form and note the number. A rep counts when your elbows bend to about 90°; that is also KINETIC's default, so the app's count is an honest test.

Add about 10 to the daily total each week until you reach 100. If your form slips, keep the total the same for another week.

Rules that keep it sustainable

  • Quality first: full range, straight body. Ten good reps beat twenty half reps.
  • Rest between sets as long as you need. The goal is the daily total, not speed.
  • Take a day off when your shoulders, elbows or wrists ache. A streak isn't worth an injury.
  • Easier variations, like hands on a bench or knees on the floor, are fine while you build up.

Track it with KINETIC

  1. Set your daily goal for push-ups to your current total (100 when you get there).
  2. Put the phone on the floor beside you and do each set. Every rep that reaches 90° is counted and added to today's total.
  3. When you hit the goal, the day is checked in on your calendar and your streak grows.
