Beginner bodyweight plan

Train 3 days a week with a rest day in between, for example Monday, Wednesday and Friday. In week 1 do 2 sets each of 15 jumping jacks, 8 squats, 5 push-ups (on your knees is fine) and 10 glute bridges. Add a set or a few reps each week, and add lunges from week 3. Each session takes about 20 minutes.

One session, step by step

  1. Warm up for 3 minutes: march in place, roll your shoulders, swing your arms.
  2. Standing exercises first. Stand the phone upright 2–3 m away, facing you, and do the jumping jacks and squats. For lunges, turn sideways to the phone.
  3. Then the floor exercises. Move the phone to the floor beside you and do the push-ups and glute bridges.
  4. Rest 60–90 seconds between sets. Take longer if you need to; the goal is good reps, not speed.
  5. Cool down: walk around for a minute and stretch your legs, hips and chest.

If push-ups are too hard

Do them on your knees, keeping a straight line from shoulders to knees. KINETIC still counts a rep when your elbows bend to about 90°, as long as your body stays close to horizontal. Push-ups against a wall or a high bench won't be counted. How push-ups are counted.

How to move on

  • Finished every set with good form? Go to the next week.
  • Couldn't finish, or your form fell apart in the last reps? Repeat the week. Taking 6 weeks instead of 4 is fine.
  • Mild muscle soreness for a day or two is normal in the first weeks. Sharp pain or joint pain is not: stop and rest.
  • Missed a session? Just do the next one. Don't squeeze two into one day.
  • If you have a health condition or an injury, or haven't exercised in a long time, check with a doctor first.

After week 4

Keep going with week 4 and add reps, or pick a challenge: the 30-day squat challenge or 100 push-ups a day.

Track it with KINETIC

At the start of each week, set the daily goal for each exercise to the total in the table (for week 1: 30 jumping jacks, 16 squats, 10 push-ups, 20 glute bridges).
