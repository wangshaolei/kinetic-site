GUIDE

How on-device pose detection works

Each camera frame is analysed on your phone by Google ML Kit, turned into body landmark positions and then discarded. KINETIC counts reps from joint angles calculated from those landmarks. No video is recorded or uploaded, and workout records stay on the phone.

What happens to each frame

  1. The camera captures a frame. Frames that arrive while the phone is still busy are skipped rather than queued, so counting never lags behind you.
  2. ML Kit finds your body. Google ML Kit's pose detection model, which ships inside the app, locates 33 body landmarks such as shoulders, elbows, hips, knees and ankles.
  3. The frame is discarded. Only the landmark positions are kept, as numbers. The picture itself is never written to storage.
  4. Joint angles are calculated. For a squat, KINETIC measures the angle at your knee between thigh and shin. Positions are smoothed to reduce jitter.
  5. A rep is counted. When the angle passes the completion threshold and comes back to the start position, the counter goes up by one.

What leaves your phone

DataLeaves the phone?
Camera video and imagesNever
Workout recordsNever. Stored on this phone only, per account
Google account name, email, photoNever sent to our servers. Stored on this phone after you sign in
Anonymous usage stats and crash reports (Google Firebase)Yes, unless you turn them off in Settings. No video, account or email
ML Kit anonymous usage stats (model version, run time)Yes, sent to Google by ML Kit. Never images

Because detection runs on the phone, counting also works without an internet connection.
