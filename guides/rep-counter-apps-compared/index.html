Which one fits you

  • You use an iPhone: GoodGYM. KINETIC doesn't have an iOS version yet.
  • You want the most exercises, programs or gym logging: MyRepsCount.
  • You want form and tempo feedback for curls and presses: REP AI.
  • You want daily push-up, squat and crunch challenges: Repletic.
  • You want an app with no ads, no purchases and no sign-up, and you want to set how deep each rep must go: KINETIC.

Where KINETIC falls short

  • Android only for now.
  • 11 exercises and no custom exercises. MyRepsCount and GoodGYM count more.
  • It checks range of motion (the joint angle), not form errors or tempo.
  • No training programs, games or smartwatch support.

How this comparison was made

  • Only facts written on each store page are used. Where a page doesn't say something, the table says "Not stated".
  • Privacy entries are what each developer declares in Google Play's Data safety section or Apple's privacy label. Stores don't verify them.
  • We didn't test how accurately the other apps count, so there's no accuracy column.
  • Prices and features change. Check the store page before you install. If something here is wrong, email leishaolen@gmail.com and we'll fix it.
