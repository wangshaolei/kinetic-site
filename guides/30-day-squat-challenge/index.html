30-day squat challenge

Start with 20 squats on day 1, add 5 each training day and rest every fourth day. You finish with 130 squats on day 30. Split each day into sets of 10–20 and go down until your thighs are about parallel to the floor.

How to do each squat

Feet shoulder-width apart, sit your hips back, chest up, heels down. Go down until your thighs are about parallel to the floor and stand all the way up. KINETIC counts a rep at a knee angle of 110° or less (the default when you face the camera) and waits for you to stand back up past 160°. More on how squats are counted.

Tips

  • Split each day into sets of 10–20 with a short rest in between.
  • Rest days matter. Muscles get stronger while you recover.
  • Missed a day? Pick up where you left off instead of doubling up.
  • Knee pain is a signal to stop and check your form, not to push through.

Track it with KINETIC

Set your squat daily goal to the day's number, stand the phone upright 2–3 m in front of you, and squat. Every counted rep adds to today's total, and hitting the goal checks the day in on your calendar.
