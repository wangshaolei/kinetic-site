01 · How it counts

It watches your joint angles, not a stopwatch.

KINETIC counts a squat when your knee angle drops to 110° or less and comes back above 160°. Half reps don't count, and the app tells you to go lower.

  1. Stand tallKnee above 160°, ready position.
  2. Go down to 110° or lessThe frame turns green when the rep is deep enough.
  3. Stand back up = +1Above 160° again, the rep is counted.

06 · Phone setup

Where to put your phone.

Most miscounts come from the camera not seeing your whole body. Good light, full body in frame.

Squat · face the camera SQUAT

Phone upright at waist height, 2–3 m away. Head to toe in frame.

Push-up · side view, low PUSH-UP

Phone on the floor beside you, side-on, so it sees you from head to feet.

08 · FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does KINETIC count reps?
KINETIC tracks your joints with the phone camera and measures joint angles. A squat counts when your knee angle drops to 110° or less and comes back above 160°. Pose detection runs on the phone.
Is my video uploaded?
No. Video is processed on your phone and never recorded or uploaded. Workout records are stored only on this device.
The count seems off. What can I do?
Keep your whole body in frame with good lighting. Face the camera for squats; for push-ups, put the phone on the floor beside you so it sees you from the side. You can also adjust the angle thresholds in the app. See phone setup for every exercise.
Do I need to sign in?
No. Guest mode counts reps for every exercise but doesn't save workouts. Sign in with Google to save workouts on this phone, track check-ins and earn badges.
Will my workouts move to a new phone?
Workouts are stored only on your phone, with no cloud backup. You can export them from Settings › Data & storage and keep the file yourself.
