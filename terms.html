TERMS · v1.1

Terms of use

These are the same terms shown in the app (v1.1).

Exercise carries risk. Rep counts are for reference only and don't replace professional guidance. Check your own condition before training, and stop right away if you feel pain, dizziness or discomfort.

The service

KINETIC uses your phone's camera to track body pose and count reps for exercises like push-ups and squats, with check-ins, badges and hero cards. Tracking and counting happen on your phone.

Accounts and sign-in

You can sign in with a Google account to keep workouts on this phone under your account. Keep your Google account secure; you can sign out or delete your account and data at any time.

Guest mode

Every feature works without signing in, but workouts aren't saved, are cleared when you close the app, and don't count toward check-ins or badges.

Acceptable use

Use the app in a safe place with enough room to move, and make sure the phone is set down securely. Don't interfere with the app, including by cracking or reverse engineering it.

Health and exercise risk

This app isn't a medical device and doesn't give medical advice. Figures such as calories are estimates. If you have heart conditions, joint injuries, are pregnant or have other health concerns, exercise under a doctor's guidance.

Intellectual property

The app's design, logos and content belong to shaolei wang. You're free to save and share the hero cards you create.

Disclaimer and liability

Results can be affected by lighting, camera angle, obstructions and more, so we can't guarantee counts are exact. To the extent permitted by law, we aren't liable for indirect losses from using the app.

Changes and contact

We'll let you know in the app when these terms change. Questions? Email leishaolen@gmail.com.
