Squat counter

KINETIC counts one squat when your knee angle drops to 110° or less and then comes back to 160° or more. Standing, facing the camera.

How KINETIC counts squats

The knee angle is the angle at your knee between thigh and shin (hip–knee–ankle). A standard squat goes down until your thighs are parallel to the floor, a knee angle of about 90°. When you face the camera your thighs point towards it and the measured angle reads larger, so the default is 110°.

Both sides must reach the angle for the rep to count.

Where to put your phone

  1. Stand the phone upright at about waist height.
  2. Step back 2–3 m so your whole body, head to feet, is in frame.
  3. Face the camera with your feet about shoulder-width apart.

Good form

  • Feet about shoulder-width apart, toes turned out slightly.
  • Sit your hips back and down; keep your chest up and heels on the floor.
  • Stand all the way up between reps. Stopping halfway won't reach 160° and the next rep won't count.

Why a rep might not count

  • Your knees didn't bend to 110°. Go a little lower; the app tells you when you're not deep enough.
  • You didn't stand back up past 160° before the next squat.
  • Part of your body is out of frame or hidden. KINETIC pauses and shows a hint; those frames are skipped, and when you're back in view it carries on without counting the same rep twice.
  • The room is dark or there is a bright window behind you. Turn on a light and face away from the window.

Too strict or too loose?

Open the angle panel on the tracking screen and drag the thresholds. The defaults follow standard form with a little margin; beginners can loosen them, and you can tighten them as you get stronger. Each exercise keeps its own setting.
