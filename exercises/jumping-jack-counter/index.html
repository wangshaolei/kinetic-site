JUMPING JACK · AI REP COUNTER

Jumping jack counter

KINETIC counts one jumping jack when your shoulder angle opens to 150° or more and then comes back to 40° or less. Standing, facing the camera.

How KINETIC counts jumping jacks

The shoulder angle is the angle at your shoulder between torso and upper arm (hip–shoulder–elbow). Your arms go from your sides (a shoulder angle of about 15°) to overhead (about 170°), both arms together. KINETIC counts at 150° and waits for your arms to come back below 40°.

Both sides must reach the angle for the rep to count.

Where to put your phone

  1. Stand the phone upright at about waist height.
  2. Step back 2–3 m so your whole body, head to feet, is in frame.
  3. Face the camera with your feet about shoulder-width apart.

Good form

  • Face the camera so both arms are visible.
  • Raise your arms all the way overhead on every rep.
  • Bring your arms right back down to your sides.

Why a rep might not count

  • One arm stopped short of overhead. Both arms must reach 150°.
  • Your hands left the top of the frame. Step back a little.
  • Part of your body is out of frame or hidden. KINETIC pauses and shows a hint; those frames are skipped, and when you're back in view it carries on without counting the same rep twice.
  • The room is dark or there is a bright window behind you. Turn on a light and face away from the window.

Too strict or too loose?

Open the angle panel on the tracking screen and drag the thresholds. The defaults follow standard form with a little margin; beginners can loosen them, and you can tighten them as you get stronger. Each exercise keeps its own setting.
