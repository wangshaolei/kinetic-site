FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does KINETIC count reps?
KINETIC tracks your joints with the phone camera and measures joint angles. A squat counts when your knee angle drops to 110° or less and comes back above 160°. Pose detection runs on the phone.
Is my video uploaded?
No. Video is processed on your phone and never recorded or uploaded. Workout records are stored only on this device.
The count seems off. What can I do?
Keep your whole body in frame with good lighting. Face the camera for squats; for push-ups, put the phone on the floor beside you so it sees you from the side. You can also adjust the angle thresholds in the app. See phone setup for every exercise.
Do I need to sign in?
No. Guest mode counts reps for every exercise but doesn't save workouts. Sign in with Google to save workouts on this phone, track check-ins and earn badges.
Will my workouts move to a new phone?
Workouts are stored only on your phone, with no cloud backup. You can export them from Settings › Data & storage and keep the file yourself.
Which exercises can KINETIC count?
11 exercises: push-ups, squats, lunges, sit-ups, jumping jacks, glute bridges, leg raises, pull-ups, bicep curls, lateral raises and overhead presses. See how each one is counted.
Is KINETIC free?
Yes. KINETIC is free to download and has no ads.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Pose detection runs on the phone, so counting works without an internet connection. Signing in with Google needs a connection.
Is there an iPhone version?
Not yet. KINETIC is currently available on Android only.
Can I make counting stricter or looser?
Yes. Open the angle panel on the tracking screen and drag the thresholds. Each exercise keeps its own setting.
Can I use the front or back camera?
Both. Switch cameras with the button at the top of the tracking screen. With the front camera you can watch yourself on screen.
Does it call out my reps?
Yes. Voice feedback calls out your reps, so you don't need to look at the screen.
Can I export my workouts?
Yes. In Settings › Data & storage you can export your workouts as CSV (opens in any spreadsheet app) or JSON (full backup).
How do I delete my account and data?
In the app, go to Settings › Data & storage › Delete account and data. Uninstalling also deletes everything on the phone. More options.
